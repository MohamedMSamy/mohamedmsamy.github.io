001ASURT · Control Subteam

F1TENTH Autonomous Race Car — ASURT RoboRacer

Team project building and racing a 1/10th-scale autonomous car for ASURT's F1TENTH team, competing at the IFAC World Congress 2026 in Busan, South Korea. The stack (forked from ETH Zürich's ForzaETH Race Stack) runs on ROS 2 with dedicated packages for perception, planning, control, and state estimation — Cartographer for SLAM, a VESC motor controller interface, and trajectory optimization for the racing line, all developed and tested first in the AutoDRIVE simulator before moving to hardware.

My contributions: a Follow-the-Gap reactive navigation node (Python, ROS 2) in the controller package, VESC 6 EDU motor controller integration with the steering servo, the Dockerized development environment, and perception debugging — tracking down LiDAR false-positive detections from wet-track reflections and solar blinding during on-track testing.

0022nd Place · 6/6 Tasks · Zero Penalties

Modular Omni-Wheel Mobile Manipulator

Built a fully integrated mobile manipulator for the Spring 2026 Mechatronics Omni-Challenge at Ain Shams University, placing 2nd overall with a perfect task score 6 out of 6 tasks completed, zero penalties.

The robot combines a holonomic omni-wheel base with a modular 5-DOF arm and gripper, operating across two modes: manual wireless teleoperation for track navigation and a fully autonomous pick-and-place phase using QR code detection for colour-coded cube sorting and delivery to matching drop-off zones.

The system runs on three ESP32 controllers (base + arm + vision) communicating over ESP-NOW, with the ESP32-CAM and quirc library handling QR decoding and a WebSocket layer coordinating real-time inter-module state.
